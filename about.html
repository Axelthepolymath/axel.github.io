About

I’m Axel. Axel is my second name and the name I use professionally and personally. My full legal name is Mohammed Wael El Hajj. I am an independent polymath researcher who works across disciplines to investigate and solve complex problems. My approach is simple: when I encounter a difficult problem, I go to the field where the problem belongs and work from its foundations. If the problem is mathematical, I turn to mathematics. If it is scientific, I investigate it through science. If it requires philosophical reasoning, I approach it through philosophy. When appropriate, I bring these disciplines together. I don’t see disciplines as boundaries. I see them as tools for understanding problems—and I move between them in search of solutions.
